# Remote Work and IT Policy

## Remote work
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval. Core collaboration hours are 11:00 to 16:00 IST, during which employees must be reachable on Slack. Fully remote arrangements need written approval from the department head.

## Equipment
Each employee receives a company laptop on joining. Laptops must use full-disk encryption and automatic screen lock after 5 minutes. Personal devices may only access email and Slack, never customer data.

## Security
Passwords must be at least 12 characters and changed every 90 days. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all company systems. Report lost devices or suspected phishing emails to security@company.example within 1 hour.

## Expense reimbursement
Work-from-home internet costs up to 1,000 INR per month are reimbursable with a bill. Submit expense claims in the finance portal by the 25th of each month. Claims above 5,000 INR need manager approval.
