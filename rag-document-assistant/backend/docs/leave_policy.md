# Leave Policy

## Annual leave
Every full-time employee gets 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues at 2 days per month. Up to 10 unused days can be carried forward to the next year; anything above that expires on 31 March.

## Sick leave
Employees get 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required if sick leave exceeds 2 consecutive days. Sick leave cannot be carried forward.

## Parental leave
Mothers receive 26 weeks of paid maternity leave. Fathers receive 2 weeks of paid paternity leave, to be taken within 6 months of the child's birth.

## How to apply
Submit leave requests in the HR portal at least 3 working days in advance for planned leave. Your manager approves or rejects the request within 2 working days. For emergencies, inform your manager by phone or message first and regularise in the portal later.
